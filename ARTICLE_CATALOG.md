# Article Catalog — Florist.co.id

## Scope and domain boundary

This catalog turns the audited Florist.co.id structure into a buyer-to-recipient florist knowledge system. It supports the existing product and service hubs without turning informational pages into disguised sales pages. It excludes city-name permutations, unsupported nationwide or same-day promises, invented prices, stock claims, medical advice, and unverified sustainability claims. Floral.id may independently cover overlapping subjects from its commerce, craft, or partner-network viewpoint; overlap between separate domains is not cannibalization.

## Catalog statistics

- 18 parent topics and 108 planned articles.
- Six distinct articles per topic; each owns one reader job and a non-overlapping boundary.
- 12 P0 first-wave assets, followed by bounded product, operations, and specialist-reference waves.
- Existing commercial hubs remain the owners of transactional product and service intent.

## Priority legend

- `P0`: first bounded publication cluster.
- `P1`: high-value supporting knowledge needed soon after the hub.
- `P2`: specialist depth after the core system is internally linked.
- `P3`: historical or advanced reference material requiring extra evidence.
